      How the numbers are worked out
      
      
      • Gap in metres = speed (km/h) ÷ 3.6 × time (s).
      • Average acceleration = (v₂² − v₁²) ÷ (2 × L), speeds in m/s, constant over the whole lane.
      • Sideways acceleration on a curve = v² ÷ R. Friction needed = sideways acceleration ÷ 9.81.
      • Stopping distance = v × t + v² ÷ (2a).
      • Work-zone taper = W × S ÷ 1.6. The units of this formula are still to be confirmed.
      
    
      Limits
      
      
      • Not legal or safety advice, and not an official requirement of any authority.
      • Country data was compiled up to 9 November 2026. Some items rest on summaries or are unverified, and are labelled.
      • Pass or fail limits are not set. Nothing without a source is invented; such items are marked as to be set.
      • Merge and lane-change gap means come from a PoliTO dissertation, not Ontario data.
      • Spacing between scenario steps is a proposal.
      • Not scientifically validated.
